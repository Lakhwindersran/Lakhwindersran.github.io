How to Reach Us

🚌

By Bus

Regular Punjab Roadways buses run from Bathinda city to Sangat Mandi. Alight at Sangat Mandi bus stop, school is 500m walking distance.

🚗

By Car / Auto

Sangat Mandi is approx. 30 km from Bathinda city centre. Take the Bathinda–Sangat road. The school is located in the main village area.

🗺️

Nearby Landmark

The school is located near the main road of Sangat Mandi village. Ask locals for "Sarkari School" – it is well-known in the area.
