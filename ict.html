Computer Laboratory

Our ICT Infrastructure

The Information and Communication Technology (ICT) lab at Government Senior Secondary School, Sangat Mandi, is one of the key facilities that distinguishes our institution. Set up under the Punjab Government's digital education initiative, the lab equips our students with essential digital skills.

The lab houses 30 modern computer systems with the latest hardware and software configurations. High-speed broadband internet connectivity ensures students have access to digital learning resources, e-DIKSHA content, and NCERT portals.

Under the expert guidance of our three dedicated Computer Science teachers, students learn topics ranging from basic computing to programming, digital literacy, and practical application development.

🎓 Curriculum Integration

  • ▶Computer Science as a compulsory subject for Class 8–10 students
  • ▶Computer Applications offered as elective subject in Class 11–12
  • ▶PSEB Board practical examinations conducted in the ICT lab
  • ▶Regular typing, word processing and spreadsheet practice sessions
  • ▶Introduction to internet safety, cybersecurity awareness programmes
