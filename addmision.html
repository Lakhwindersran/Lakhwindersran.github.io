Admission Guidelines

Eligibility & Required Documents

✅ Eligibility Criteria

  • 📌Age limit: As per PSEB norms for each class
  • 📌Must have passed the previous class from a recognised board/school
  • 📌Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous school is mandatory (Class 7 onwards)
  • 📌Aadhaar Card is mandatory for all students
  • 📌Preference given to students from Sangat Mandi and nearby villages

📁 Required Documents

  • 📄Birth Certificate (Original + 1 Photocopy)
  • 🪪Aadhaar Card of Student (Original + 1 Photocopy)
  • 📋Previous Class Marksheet / Report Card
  • 📑Transfer Certificate from previous school
  • 🏷️Caste/Category Certificate (SC/OBC/BPL if applicable)
  • 🖼️4 recent passport size photographs of student
