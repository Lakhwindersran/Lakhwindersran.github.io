About the Library

Our School Library

In the supervision of Smt Jaspal Kaur library Attendant our school have a well-stocked repository of knowledge that caters to students of all classes from 6 to 12. The library is designed to foster a love for reading and independent learning.

With over 5,000 books covering all subjects taught in the school curriculum (PSEB), reference books, encyclopaedias, fiction, and motivational literature, the library serves as a second classroom for our students.

The library is open from Monday to Saturday during school hours. Students can borrow books for home reading under the library issue system supervised by the librarian in-charge.

📖 Library Timings

Monday – Saturday: 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM
Break Period: Reading & borrowing allowed
Book Issue: 1 book per student at a time (7 days)

Facilities

Reading Facilities & Digital Resources

🪑

Dedicated Reading Hall

A quiet, well-lit reading room with over 40 seats on first floor for students to read and study. Separate sections for senior and junior students ensure a comfortable environment.

📰

Daily Newspapers

Multiple daily newspapers in Punjabi, Hindi and English are subscribed to keep students updated with current affairs. Essential for competitive exam preparation.

💻

Digital Learning Corner

6 dedicated computer terminals in the library provide access to e-books, NCERT resources, and educational videos under teacher supervision.

📺

Educational Content Viewing

A smart TV in the library is used to screen educational documentaries, science programmes and career guidance videos for students.

🔍

Reference Section

A dedicated reference corner with encyclopaedias, dictionaries, atlases, and competitive exam preparation books for Class 10 and 12 board exam students.

📚

Book Issue System

Students can borrow one book at a time for up to 7 days through a structured issue-and-return register maintained by the library teacher in-charge.

Guidelines

Library Rules & Regulations

✅ Do's

  • ✓ Maintain complete silence in the reading room
  • ✓ Return borrowed books within 7 days
  • ✓ Handle books with care, keep pages clean
  • ✓ Show your school ID card when borrowing books
  • ✓ Report damaged or missing books to the librarian
  • ✓ Put books back in their proper place after reading

❌ Don'ts

  • ✗ Do not bring food or drinks into the library
  • ✗ Do not write or highlight in library books
  • ✗ Do not tear pages or damage books in any way
  • ✗ Do not use mobile phones inside the library
  • ✗ Do not talk loudly or disturb other students
  • ✗ Do not remove books without proper issue procedure
