About the Scheme

PM Poshan Abhiyan (Mid Day Meal)

The Pradhan Mantri Poshan Shakti Nirman (PM POSHAN), formerly known as the Mid Day Meal Scheme, is a flagship programme of the Government of India implemented in all government and government-aided schools across the country.

At Government Senior Secondary School, Sangat Mandi, this scheme benefits over 300 students daily, providing them with a hot, nutritious cooked meal during school hours. The meal is prepared by trained cooks following the guidelines issued by the Punjab government under supervision of the school authorities.

The programme aims to address issues of hunger, malnutrition, and school dropout, while also improving attendance and enrollment, especially among children from economically weaker sections.

Daily Meal Standards

🌾Cereals (Wheat / Rice) – 100g per student
🫘Pulses / Dal – 20g per student
🥦Seasonal Vegetables – 50g per student
🫙Cooking Oil / Spices as per norms
🧂Iodised Salt – as per requirement

Impact

Benefits for Students

The Mid Day Meal Programme has transformed school attendance and student health across Punjab.

📈

Improved Attendance

Regular meals act as an incentive, significantly reducing absenteeism especially among students from disadvantaged families.

🧠

Better Concentration

Nutritious midday meal improves cognitive functioning, helping students stay alert and focused during afternoon sessions.

💪

Improved Nutrition

Provides essential nutrients, proteins and calories to children who may otherwise go undernourished.

👫

Social Harmony

Students of all castes and religions eat together, promoting equality, unity and national integration.

🎓

Higher Enrollment

The meal scheme has played a significant role in encouraging parents to send their children to school regularly.

👨‍👩‍👧

Gender Equality

Helps in increasing girl enrollment and retention in school, especially in rural areas of Bathinda.

Accountability

Quality Monitoring & Transparency

🏫

SMC Oversight

School Management Committee (SMC) members regularly inspect the kitchen, quality of ingredients and meal distribution.

📋

Daily Register

A daily register of student count, food prepared and served is maintained and submitted to the DEO office monthly.

🧪

Food Quality Check

Teachers and principal taste food before it is served to students, ensuring standards are always maintained.
