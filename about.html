Our Roots

School History

Government Senior Secondary School, Sangat Mandi, is one of the esteemed government educational institutions of Bathinda district, Punjab. Established under the aegis of the Punjab School Education Department, the school has been serving the educational needs of the local community for several decades.

Over the years, the institution has grown from a small primary school into a fully-fledged Senior Secondary School, now offering education from Class 6 to Class 12 across Science, Commerce and Arts streams. The school operates under the Punjab School Education Board (PSEB), Mohali.

The school has consistently produced outstanding results and has been a beacon of hope and opportunity for students from Sangat Mandi and the surrounding villages of Bathinda.

Vision & Mission

🎯

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence that nurtures curious, capable, and compassionate citizens who contribute positively to the society and nation.

  • Provide quality education accessible to all children
  • Develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Foster national pride and civic responsibility
  • Bridge the rural-urban education divide
  • Empower students with digital literacy
🚀

Our Mission

To deliver holistic, inclusive, and innovative education that prepares every student for the challenges of the 21st century.

  • Ensure no child is left behind due to financial constraints
  • Train teachers with modern pedagogical techniques
  • Integrate technology into classroom learning
  • Promote physical fitness and mental wellbeing
  • Build strong school-community partnerships
